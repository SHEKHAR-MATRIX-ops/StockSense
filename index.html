📦 Why this site exists

StockSense — inventory, without the spreadsheet chaos

Most small teams track stock in scattered Excel sheets — no one knows what's actually on the shelf, what's already promised to a customer, or what's about to run out. StockSense was built to fix exactly that: one place to receive goods, ship them out, move them between warehouses, and see live stock levels — so nothing gets over-sold or forgotten.

  • 📥Receipts — log incoming stock from suppliers into a warehouse.
  • 📤Delivery — reserve and ship stock out to customers.
  • 🔁Adjustments — move or correct stock between locations.
  • 📊Dashboard & Stock — see totals, value, and low/out-of-stock alerts at a glance.
